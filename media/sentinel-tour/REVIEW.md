# Review

Creative direction: a factual narrated product tour using actual frontend captures. All six roles are explained; approval, application, and verification remain separate. The video ends at the existing introduction and learning demo. A silent marketing reel and a synthetic presenter were considered unnecessary for this request.

Measured: 94.25 seconds, thirteen scenes, generated local narration WAV timing, scene-level captions, zero HyperFrames lint/runtime/motion/contrast/audio errors and no craft warnings. Overlap notices are informational at the intentional 0.4-second scene dissolves. Sound assets were validated programmatically; voice quality was not auditioned by listening.

Inspected: individual frames and a contact sheet covering all thirteen chapters, at 3, 10, 17, 23, 30, 37, 44, 52, 60, 76, 83, 90 and 94 seconds. Screens and explanatory text are visible. The screenshots contain small operational text intended for fullscreen viewing; the large role title and explanation provide the small-player summary. The final web copy is H.264/AAC, 1280×720, 7,241,564 bytes, 94.25 seconds, with a measured mean level of -21.2 dB and peak of -2.6 dB. Native playback, seeking, captions and transcript fallback were verified in Chromium.

Limitations: this is an annotated screenshot tour with camera movement and narration, not a recording of live backend execution or a mouse-driven training session. Existing interactive onboarding and the isolated demo provide hands-on exploration. The voice is local Microsoft Zira speech synthesis. Captions are timed at scene/utterance level rather than per word.
